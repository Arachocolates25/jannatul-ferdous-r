About me

I'm a young entrepreneur and creator who likes to build things from scratch. I started with a chocolate brand, then moved into websites and cinematic video, and I learn most by doing the work myself.

I work by three rules: start small, stay curious, and care about every detail. I believe a small thing done properly is worth more than a big thing done halfway.

My goal is to grow Ara Chocolates into a brand that Bangladesh remembers, and to keep making work that looks as good as it feels.

Start small. Stay curious. Make every detail count.

What I do

  • Founder

    I run Ara Chocolates. I handle the brand, the look, the product photos and reaching out to businesses who may sell or order our chocolate.

  • Web creator

    I design and code websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I focus on pages that load fast, work on phones and are easy to read.

  • Video editor and colour grader

    I edit in CapCut and grade in DaVinci Resolve. I give flat footage a film look, from space shots to cars and food.

  • Student

    I study ICT, web design and business, and I keep learning new tools by building small projects of my own.
