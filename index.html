About

I'm a young entrepreneur and creator who likes to build things from scratch, and I learn most by doing the work myself.

I started with a chocolate brand, then moved into websites and cinematic video. Each one taught me the same lesson: small details decide whether something feels ordinary or well made.

My goal is to grow Ara Chocolates into a brand that Bangladesh remembers, and to keep making work that looks as good as it feels. In my free time I read about astrophysics, history and cars.
