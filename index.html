About

My Story

I'm Rayhan — a young entrepreneur and creator from Khulna, Bangladesh, who loves building things from scratch. From a chocolate brand to websites and cinematic video, I learn by doing.

My thinking is simple: start small, stay curious, and make every detail count. I study ICT and design, explore astrophysics, history and cars in my free time, and I'm always planning the next thing.

My goal is to grow Ara Chocolates into a brand Bangladesh remembers — and to keep creating work that looks as good as it feels.
